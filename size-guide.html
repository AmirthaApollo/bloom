How to measure

  • Bust · around the fullest part, tape level and not too tight.
  • Waist · around the narrowest part, usually above the navel.
  • Hip · around the fullest part, feet together.
  • Foot · stand on paper, mark heel and toe, measure the distance.

Second-hand sizing can vary. When in doubt, message the seller for the exact measurements of the item.
