Our story

Bloom started in a hostel corridor, with a suitcase of clothes that no longer fit and a friend who wanted exactly those jeans. We realised the best marketplace for students was already on campus, just scattered across group chats.

So we built one place for it · a calmer, kinder way to pass things on. Every listing belongs to a real student, and every purchase keeps something out of a landfill.

Sustainability

The most sustainable thing you own is the thing you already have. Every second-hand handoff on Bloom avoids new production, new packaging and new shipping.

  • No shipping, no plastic · pickups happen on foot.
  • Buying pre-owned extends an item's life by years.
  • Student-made and handmade listings keep money on campus.

Privacy & terms

Bloom is a frontend demo, so everything here runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Your bag, wishlist, listings and orders are stored locally on this device only.
  • There is no account system and we do not collect personal data.
  • Emails and payments are simulated for the demo · no real messages or charges.
  • Be kind, describe your items honestly, and meet in public campus spots.
