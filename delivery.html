No shipping, no packaging · just a friendly on-campus handoff.

How pickup works

Bloom is a student-to-student marketplace, so everything happens on campus. There is no courier and nothing to unpack.

  1. You order and pay securely · the item is reserved and removed from the marketplace.
  2. We email the seller straight away so they know it sold.
  3. You and the seller agree a time and place · the library steps, the student commons, between classes.
  4. You meet, check the item, and confirm the handoff. Payment is released to the seller.

Timing

  • Most handoffs happen within the same week.
  • Sellers usually reply within a few hours during term time.
  • Stuck? Track your order or contact us.

Returns

Second-hand things are not always perfect, so we keep returns simple and fair.

  • If an item is not as described, raise a return within 24 hours of the handoff.
  • Send us photos of the item and the listing · we compare them with the seller.
  • If the listing was inaccurate, you get a full refund and we follow up with the seller.
  • Change of mind? Message the seller · many are happy to re-list the item for you.

Meeting safely

  • Meet in public, well-lit campus spots · the library, the commons, the cafeteria.
  • Check the item before you confirm the handoff.
  • Keep the conversation inside Bloom so we can help if anything goes wrong.
