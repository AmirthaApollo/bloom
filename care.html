Care guide

Pre-owned things last longer with a little care.

Clothing

  • Wash cold and inside-out to keep prints and colours alive.
  • Air-dry where you can · it is gentler on fabric and on the planet.
  • Store knits folded, not hung, so they keep their shape.
  • Turn delicates inside-out in a mesh bag, or hand-wash.

Leather & bags

  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth after use, especially in the monsoon.
  • Condition leather now and then to stop it drying out.
  • Stuff bags with paper to hold their shape when stored.
  • Keep them out of direct sun and away from radiators.

Jewellery

  • Keep pieces dry · take them off before showers and swimming.
  • Store separately in a pouch or box to avoid scratches.
  • Polish gently with a soft cloth, not abrasive materials.

Dorm & decor

  • Dust ceramics and wood with a dry microfibre cloth.
  • Keep candles out of direct sun so they do not sweat.
  • Wash cushion covers cold and line-dry to protect prints.
