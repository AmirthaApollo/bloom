How it works

Five steps from listing to handoff. No shipping, no strangers off campus.

1

List your item

Add your product details, photos, price and category. It appears in the marketplace instantly.

2

A student places an order

Another student checks out. The item is reserved and becomes unavailable right away.

3

Bloom notifies you

You receive an order notification in your seller dashboard the moment it sells.

4

Coordinate delivery

We email you so you can arrange an on-campus handoff with the buyer, at a time that suits you both.

5

Item is removed

Once ordered, the item is automatically removed from the marketplace so no one else can buy it.

This is a frontend demo. Orders, notifications and emails are simulated locally. No real payments, accounts or emails.
